SPECIFICATION — ASPHALT PAVEMENT
SECTION 4.1 HOT MIX ASPHALT

4.1.1 The contractor shall furnish and place hot mix asphalt (HMA) pavement
in accordance with this specification. The mix shall be Superpave 12.5mm
with a PG 64-22 binder.

4.1.2 Compacted thickness shall be 3 inches of HMA over the prepared base.
A minimum 92% of maximum theoretical density shall be achieved, and the
compaction requirement shall be met in place.

4.1.3 Tack coat shall be applied with SS-1h emulsified asphalt before each
layer is placed.

4.1.4 The aggregate base shall be 6 inches of crushed stone compacted to
95% standard Proctor.

4.1.5 HMA shall be delivered at a temperature between 285 degrees F and
320 degrees F. Placement shall not begin when air temperature is below 50
degrees F.

4.1.6 Testing: tensile strength ratio testing shall be performed on
laboratory samples. Acceptance shall be based on coring results and
pay adjustment may apply for failing lots.

4.1.7 Typical lift thickness may be 1.5 inches for overlays; for
reference only, the Asphalt Institute recommends 2-inch minimum surface
course.

NOTE: Any conflict between this specification and the drawings shall be
reported to the engineer; the more stringent requirement governs.
